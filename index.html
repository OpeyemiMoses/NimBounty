How it works

From bounty creation to direct worker payout in five simple steps

1
CREATE

Post Bounty Task

Poster creates a task, sets reward per completion, and specifies worker slots. Instant & free to publish.

2
PROTECT

Device Lock & Claim

Workers claim tasks bound by Nimiq device ID to eliminate multi-accounting and bot farming.

3
SIGN

Complete & Sign Proof

Worker executes feedback or testing and signs submission proof message off-chain with zero gas fees.

4
VERIFY

Review Proof

Poster reviews worker's submitted proof package with 1-click approval or rejection safeguard.

5
SETTLE

Direct Instant Payout

NIM reward transfers directly from poster's connected wallet to worker's wallet with zero middleman cuts.

Built-in Protections

Every task pool ships with these safeguards

Anti-Sybil Device Caps

Hardware-bound 64-hex device signatures via Nimiq SDK ensure 1 physical device cannot farm multiple bounty slots.

Direct Wallet Payouts

Posters approve proof and transfer NIM directly to workers via Nimiq Pay native settlement.

Zero Worker Gas Costs

Workers sign proof messages off-chain via Nimiq Pay keys with 0 transaction or gas fees.

Non-Custodial Direct Payments

Funds remain in user wallets until poster approves proof and triggers payout directly to worker.

FAQ

Common questions about NimBounty

NimBounty is a micro-task and crowd-testing platform built on the Nimiq Pay Mini Apps Framework. It allows builders to post bounties for user feedback, app testing, and social campaigns while workers earn instant NIM payouts with zero transaction fees.

No! Workers do not pay any gas or transaction fees. They sign their submission proof message off-chain via Nimiq Pay keys for free.

NimBounty uses Nimiq Pay's hardware-bound requestDeviceIdentifier API. Each physical device generates a unique 64-character SHA-256 identifier, preventing a single user from creating multiple fake accounts to farm task slots.

When a task poster reviews and approves a worker's submitted proof, NIM is transferred directly from the poster's connected wallet to the worker's wallet inside Nimiq Pay with zero middleman fees.

NimBounty supports native Nimiq (NIM) payments as well as EVM tokens (USDT on Polygon, Mainnet, Base, Arbitrum) through Nimiq Pay's dual-provider engine.
