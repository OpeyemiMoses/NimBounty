How it works

From bounty creation to instant worker payout in five steps

Poster creates a task, sets reward per completion, and deposits total NIM into smart escrow in one transaction.

2

NIM reward releases immediately from escrow to the worker's wallet with zero middleman cuts.

Built-in Protections

Every task pool ships with these onchain safeguards

Anti-Sybil Device Caps

Hardware-bound 64-hex device signatures via Nimiq SDK ensure 1 physical device cannot farm multiple bounty slots.

Auto-Approve Safeguards

If a poster is inactive for >24 hours after proof submission, funds automatically release to protect worker labor.

Zero Worker Gas Costs

Workers sign proof messages off-chain; reward disbursements are covered entirely out of the poster's initial deposit.

Locked Non-Custodial Escrow

Funds are locked securely in smart contract vaults. Posters can reclaim unused slots anytime after pool expiry.

FAQ

Common questions about NimBounty

NimBounty is a micro-task and crowd-testing platform built on the Nimiq Pay Mini Apps Framework. It allows builders to post bounties for user feedback, app testing, and social campaigns while workers earn instant NIM payouts with zero transaction fees.

NimBounty uses Nimiq Pay's hardware-bound requestDeviceIdentifier API. Each physical device generates a unique 64-character SHA-256 identifier, preventing a single user from creating multiple fake accounts to farm task slots.

Every bounty includes an automatic 24-hour verification window. If the task poster does not manually review or reject your submission within 24 hours, the smart escrow contract auto-approves your submission and transfers the NIM reward to your wallet.

NimBounty supports native Nimiq (NIM) payments as well as EVM tokens (USDT on Polygon, Mainnet, Base, Arbitrum) through Nimiq Pay's dual-provider engine.
