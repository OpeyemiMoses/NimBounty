FAQ

Common questions about NimBounty

NimBounty is a micro-task and crowd-testing platform built on the Nimiq Pay Mini Apps Framework. It allows builders to post bounties for user feedback, app testing, and social campaigns while workers earn instant NIM payouts.

When a publisher creates a bounty pool, Nimiq Pay prompts them to send the total campaign NIM amount into the NimBounty Escrow Vault (`NQ73 ESCR OW...`). Once approved by the publisher, the reward is released directly from escrow to the worker's address.

Outside Nimiq Pay, the interface remains explorable and explains how to open inside Nimiq Pay Mobile App to connect your wallet.
